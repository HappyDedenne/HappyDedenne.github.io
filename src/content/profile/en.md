I am a third-year Ph.D. student at the Graduate School of Informatics, Kyoto University, advised by Prof. Takashi Sato. From 2021 to March 2026, I was advised by Prof. Hiromitsu Awano, who is now at Nagoya University.

My research focuses on energy-efficient circuits and architectures for AI computation, in particular SRAM-based computing-in-memory and lookup-table–based multiplication-free accelerators. I have taped out full-custom chips in 22&nbsp;nm and 150&nbsp;nm processes and completed two summer internships at TSMC headquarters in Hsinchu, Taiwan.

I expect to receive my Ph.D. in March 2027.
